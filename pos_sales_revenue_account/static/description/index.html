Every Point of Sale, its own Sales Revenue Account

Assign an optional Sales Revenue Account to each Point of Sale configuration. When a sale is made and the session is closed, the product sales revenue is posted to the account of that Point of Sale instead of the product / product-category income account.

Key Features

  • Per-POS revenue account — each Point of Sale can have its own income account (e.g. 411001 POS Sales Cairo, 411002 POS Sales Giza).
  • Company default — set a default account from the main Settings page; any Point of Sale without its own account uses it.
  • Per-POS override — the settings page also lets you pick a Point of Sale and assign its account directly (same pattern as Default Journals).
  • Refunds — POS refunds are reversed against the same POS-specific account.
  • Income accounts only — the selector only shows valid Income / Revenue accounts of the same company.
  • Fallback preserved — when no account is set, standard product / product-category income accounts are used, exactly as in vanilla Odoo.

Accounting behaviour

Priority for the product sales revenue line of the POS closing entry:

  1. POS-specific Sales Revenue Account
  2. Company default (from the main Settings page)
  3. Product / product-category income account (standard Odoo)

Only the revenue line is affected. Tax accounts, receivable/payment accounts, cash, bank, stock valuation, COGS, rounding and session-balance logic keep their standard behaviour, so every generated move stays balanced.

Installation

  1. Copy the pos_sales_revenue_account folder into your addons path.
  2. Restart Odoo and update the module list (Apps → Update Apps List).
  3. Install the module (Apps → POS Sales Revenue Account).

Configuration: Settings → Point of Sale → Default Sales Revenue Account (optional, company-wide), and/or Point of Sale → Configuration → Point of Sale → open a POS → Accounting → Sales Revenue Account.

Compatibility & Licence

Compatible with Odoo 18 (Community Edition). Requires point_of_sale and account.
